▸Annotation & handoff · M09-S02
Purpose
The operational spine of a delivered programme: what happened, what is signed off, what still blocks the money.
Primary user
Siti (Operations Coordinator); Finance and the MD read it.
Components used
RecordHeader (5 metrics incl. progress bar), LifecycleStepper (blocked + current states), pill tabs, warning banner, two data tables, checklist, finance panel.
Data contract
Engagement{id,opportunityId,programmeId,dates[],venue,owner,status,valueRM} · Session{id,day,date,venue,trainerId,present,total} · Participant{id,name,dept,attendance{d1,d2},status} · Checklist{item,done} · ClaimStatus{completenessPct,missingDocs[]}
Actions & policy gates
Close out (primary, blocked until claim documents complete), Run sheet, Audit trail. Attendance is read-only once locked.
States rendered
Claim blocked with two missing documents; two partial-attendance participants; deadline warning banner.
AI & approval behaviour
None generating here — this is operational truth. The Compliance Agent raised the claim-packet approval that appears in M02-S01.
Wired to
Open packet → M12-S02 · Attendance metric → M10-S06 · INV-2026-0311 → M13-S02 · Trainer → M08-S02.
Assumptions
Close out requires attendance locked + evaluation summary + certificates; the button stays visible but disabled until then.
