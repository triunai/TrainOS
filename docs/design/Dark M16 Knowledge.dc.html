Retrieval policy
Compliance answers may cite HRD Corp circulars, scheme guides and the ACM only. Internal SOPs are excluded from client-facing text. A source in Changed state is quarantined from rule extraction until its diffs are reviewed, but stays readable for search.
Monitoring
Watched sources are fetched weekly and hashed. A hash change opens a rule-change review; a fetch failure is retried three times, then flagged — the LHDN guideline has been failing since 07 Nov.
▸Annotation & handoff · M16-S05
Purpose
Make the corpus visible: what was ingested, when it was last verified, and what changed under the agents’ feet.
Primary user
System Admin; Compliance follows the changed-source link.
Components used
Page header, MetricStrip (6 cells), warning banner, data table with status chips, policy notes.
Data contract
KnowledgeSource{id,name,type,version,ingestedAt,chunks,embeddingStatus,lastCheckedAt,monitorStatus,contentHash,retrievalScopes[]}
Actions & policy gates
Add source (primary), Check now, re-ingest, open the resulting rule-change review.
States rendered
One changed source, one embedding pending, one fetch failing since 07 Nov.
AI & approval behaviour
Ingestion and embedding are agent work; the changed source shows what it produced, but nothing enters the rule registry without review.
Wired to
Changed → M12-S08 · Cited documents → M12-S07 rule drawer · Chunks → search index stats.
Assumptions
Weekly hash-based monitoring; internal SOPs are retrievable for staff answers but excluded from client-facing generation.
